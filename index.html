About the game

Kouki Adventure is a fast-paced arcade survival game. Your goal is simple: stay as high as possible and avoid the deadly electric line below.

Blocks break on contact, so every move matters. Choose the safest path, react fast, and build smart strategies with power-ups.

Core gameplay

Short, intense runs. One mistake and you drop fast. Your score reflects survival time and decisions.

Power-ups

Unlock and activate upgrades like Spring, Gem, Portal, Floating Wagon and Flying Skyboard.

Ads
