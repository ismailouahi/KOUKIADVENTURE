Effective Date:

Kouki Adventure is a mobile arcade game developed by Ismail Ouahi. This game does not collect, store, or process personal data directly.

Advertising

Kouki Adventure uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information automatically, such as:

  • Device identifiers (including Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Interaction data with ads

This data is processed by Google to provide ads (personalized or non-personalized, depending on user settings and region). For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Usage

Kouki Adventure does not:

  • Require account registration
  • Collect user names or emails
  • Store gameplay data on external servers
  • Sell personal information

Children

Kouki Adventure is not specifically directed at children under 13. Ads are served in accordance with Google AdMob policies.
